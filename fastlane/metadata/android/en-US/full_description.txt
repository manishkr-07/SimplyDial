SimplyDial redefines your mobile calling experience with unmatched privacy, accessibility, and modern power. Free from advertisements and intrusive trackers, SimplyDial is designed for seamless, crystal-clear, and effortless everyday communication.

🌟 ACCESSIBILITY & SENIOR-FRIENDLY "EASEUI"
Communication should be effortless for all ages. SimplyDial features thoughtful accessibility enhancements:
• Accidental Call Shield: Built-in Call Confirmation prompt with a large, unmistakable button before placing any call.
• Messages Joystick: One-touch radial gesture control for sending quick, pre-configured SMS replies without typing.
• Tactile Swipe Answering: Ergonomic swipe gestures to answer or decline incoming calls smoothly.
• Multilingual & Regional Support: Full Hindi localization and regional voice dial language selection.

💎 BLACK DIAMOND AMOLED THEME
Experience a stunning, true AMOLED #000000 dark theme paired with electric cyan neon accents and dynamic glassmorphic overlays. Completely optimizes battery conservation on OLED screens while providing comfortable night-time viewing.

🎙️ COMPLIANT HD CALL RECORDING
Capture important conversations in 256 kbps studio-quality AAC stereo:
• Hybrid Audio Engine: Multi-tier fallback (Mic, Voice Communication, Voice Call) ensures reliable audio capture across diverse Android hardware.
• Legal Compliance: Mandatory first-use consent modal and audible two-party start/stop beep tones satisfy regulatory requirements.
• Built-in Manager: In-call live indicator and timer, post-call review dialog, and complete recording history player with secure file sharing.

📹 BUILT-IN VIDEO CALLING (JITSI MEET)
Launch secure, open-source video conferences directly from the dialer or contacts list:
• In-Call Safety: Ongoing cellular phone calls automatically mute to prevent audio echo and hardware conflict.
• Rejoin Interception: Accidental app exits prompt a quick rejoin dialog so you never drop mid-meeting.
• Instant SMS Invites: Generate and send direct meeting links via SMS with one tap.

🎙️ INSTANT OFFLINE VOICE DIALING
Speak naturally (e.g., "Call Dad", "Dial 9876543210") to instantly connect with contacts using smart on-device fuzzy matching. Completely offline and private—no cloud AI, zero external network requests.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & ON-DEVICE
Your contacts, call logs, audio recordings, and preferences never leave your smartphone. Zero analytics, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry.
